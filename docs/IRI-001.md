HTTPS GET indirectly verifies DNS, certificate trust and transport; separate expiry warning and DNS diagnostics remain open. No POSTs, no auth attempts, no writes, no secret or response body logging. Expected authorization denials never prove successful authorization. Only public website delivery is scoped as sufficiently observed to show Operational. Other four services remain Not yet verified even when probes pass. Optional failures are retained in technical details and never drive service outage. A failed first cycle is degraded; two consecutive failures of some required probes = partial outage; all required probes repeatedly failing = major outage of observed endpoints, not proof of full backend failure. Two successful cycles restore observed status. Manual incident and active maintenance overlays affect service presentation; monitoring freshness overrides stale health. >25 minutes delayed, >90 unavailable; no missing interval enters the numerator or denominator. History is sampled required-check-set pass rates, not uptime or end-to-end success rates. All original component/daily observations are archived inside the status record; incidents and maintenance remain intact. New service IDs never reuse old counters.
